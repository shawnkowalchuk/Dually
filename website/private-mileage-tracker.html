A private mileage tracker: no account, no cloud

A mileage log is a record of everywhere you drive. Dually Trip Tracker keeps it on your phone.

What that means

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no password.
  • No server. Dually has no server to send your trips to, and no analytics or ads.
  • No internet permission. The app is not allowed to open an internet connection, so it cannot send your trips anywhere by itself.

The only ways your trips leave your phone

  • Reports you send. The monthly PDF and CSV go to your email app, and you press send.
  • Files you save. An export of all your data is saved where you choose.
  • Your own Android backup. If Google backup is on for your phone, Android copies Dually's trips and settings to your Google account. The GPS points along each trip go only to a new phone in a phone-to-phone transfer.
  • Address lookup. To turn a trip's start and end into street addresses, Dually hands those two positions to your phone's own address service, which on most phones is Google's.

The privacy policy has every detail, including the two tiles in Settings that open your email and your browser, and each permission and why it is needed.

Why it matters for a mileage log

A year of trips shows where you live, where you work, who you visit and when. Services that sync trips to their servers keep that history under their own terms. With Dually, the only copies are the ones you make.

The trade-off

  • No web dashboard. Your trips are on the phone, and in the reports and exports you make.
  • A lost phone loses what is not backed up. Keep Google backup on, or export now and then.
  • One phone. Trips are not shared between devices.

Try it

Dually is free for Android 14 or newer, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Download Dually for Android, see how to install it, or read the other guides: Bluetooth mileage tracking and a mileage logbook for the CRA.
