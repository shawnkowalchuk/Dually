Delete your online backup and account

For the optional online backup of the Dually Trip Tracker app (Dually for short), sold by 2795748 Alberta Ltd. Last changed 12 November 2026.

Who this is for

Dually needs no account. The only account is the one made when you buy the optional online backup and sign in with Google, and the only data Dually keeps off your phone is that backup. If you never bought it, Dually holds nothing about you anywhere but on your phone, and there is nothing to delete here: uninstalling Dually deletes everything it keeps.

In the app

  1. Open Dually and go to Settings.
  2. On the Online backup tile, press "Delete my online backup and account".
  3. Confirm. Your online backup and its account are deleted at once.

Without the phone

If the phone is lost, broken or no longer has Dually, email support@dually.top from the address of the Google account you signed in to the backup with, and ask for your online backup and account to be deleted. They are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done. A request from any other address is answered with a question to make sure the account is yours, so that nobody can delete someone else's backup.

What is deleted

  • Every trip, sent report and setting that was backed up from your phone.
  • The account: your Google account's email address and the account's id, as Dually's backup knew them.
  • The unlock: the backup stops uploading.

What is not deleted

  • The trips on your phone. Deleting the online backup does not touch them. To delete those too, uninstall Dually.
  • Your purchase. Google Play keeps its own record of the purchase in your Google Play account, under Google Play's terms. Dually's server keeps only a record of the purchase itself, holding no trip and no email address, so that one purchase unlocks one backup.
  • Your Google account. It is Google's, not Dually's, and is not touched.

Other ways the copy ends

  • A refund. If your purchase is refunded, uploads stop, and the online copy is deleted 30 days after the refund. The trips on your phone stay.
  • Signing out stops the uploads but deletes nothing: the copy stays until you delete it.

The privacy policy says what the online backup holds, where it is kept and who handles it.
