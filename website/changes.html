What's new

Every version of Dually and what changed in it, newest first. The same list is in the app, under Settings, Version.

The newest released version is the latest download on GitHub. To update, install it over the one you have: your trips stay.

Version 0.3.2 Not released yet

  • New
    A year-end report

    The Report screen's period now reads Month, Date range or Year. Choose Year for a report of the whole year: its first page sums up each month's Business and Personal kilometres and the year's totals, under the odometer at the start and the end of the year, and the trips follow from the next page.

  • New
    Each trip's odometer on the report

    A third switch in Report options, Show each trip's odometer, adds a line under each trip on the report with the vehicle's odometer at its start and its end, worked out from your readings and marked est. where it is not a reading you typed. The CSV gets an Odometer start and an Odometer end column.

  • New
    An odometer check when you change vehicles

    With several vehicles paired, when a trip starts in a different vehicle than last time, Dually posts a quiet notification with that vehicle's odometer as it has it, such as "Ford F-150: odometer about 120,345 km?". If someone drove it without Dually, tap it to type the reading in Settings. It makes no sound and does not pop up, as you are about to drive.

  • New
    A graph of the year

    The Trips screen has a Graph of the year button. It shows each month of the year as a point, with your Business kilometres on one line and your Personal kilometres on another. Step back to earlier years, press a month to see its figures, and read every month's figures in the list under the graph.

  • Improved
    From the month on Home to the month on Trips

    Press the month's card on Home to open that month on the Trips screen. While a trip is recorded, the card shows today first; press it to see the month, and again to open Trips. The Trips screen's month card now shows the dollars beside the number of Business trips, the same as on Home.

  • New
    Business or Personal while you drive

    While a trip is being recorded, Home's trip card says Business or Personal beside Recording: what your work hours make of it. Press it to switch, for example when you leave work early for home. The trip is saved as what you chose when it ends, and Dually never changes your choice afterwards.

  • Improved
    Change today's trips from Home

    Press one of today's trips on Home to bring up the same buttons it has on the Trips screen: Personal or Business, to switch what it is saved as, and Edit. Trips in the corner of that tile still opens the Trips screen.

  • Improved
    The rate is with the report's details

    The rate your Business kilometres are priced at, for reference on Home, the widget and the Android Auto screen, is now set in Settings under Report for the accountant. The Home-screen widget's tile stays open again, with its switch at the top.

  • New
    Report options

    The Report screen has a Report options tile with two switches, kept for every report you make. List Personal trips too puts your Personal trips in the report beside the Business ones, marked Personal and in grey, without changing any Business total, and adds a Category column to the CSV. Show the Business use percentage says what share of the period's driving was Business.

  • Improved
    Battery Saver is up to you

    Setup no longer asks you to keep the phone's Battery Saver off. While it is on, Home says what it can cost: Android may limit GPS and background work, so a trip may be missed or cut short. And before your truck is paired, Setup no longer sends you to Android's battery list: pairing the truck usually takes care of that by itself.

  • Improved
    A shorter Settings screen

    Most Settings tiles now fold away under their title and a line that says how they are set, such as "Work schedule: 5 days, 42.5 h a week". Press a tile to open it, and again to close it. Setup, the truck and the odometer stay open. A tile that is missing something, such as the name for the report, starts open so you can see what to fill in.

  • Improved
    Personal kilometres on each day

    On the Trips screen, a day with a Personal trip now says what its Personal trips add up to, after its Business kilometres: "4 trips · 48.2 km business · 12.0 km personal".

  • New
    Adjust trip distances to match your odometer

    Settings has a Distance adjustment under Shortest trip that counts. If Dually's trips come out a little shorter or longer than your truck's odometer, raise or lower them half a percent at a time, up to 5 percent either way. It applies to the trip being recorded and every trip after it; trips that have ended keep their distance.

  • New
    Delete a sent month's GPS points

    Every trip keeps its route as GPS points, and they add up to about 80 MB a year. Once a month's report is sent and the month has ended, the Report screen offers to delete that month's points, saying how much room they take. Every trip keeps its distance, times and addresses, and the report stays as it was; only the route goes. Press Keep and Dually does not ask about that month again. Settings has a new GPS points tile that shows how much room the points take, and deletes the points of every month whose report you sent. To keep a copy first, use Export.

  • Improved
    The one-year party waits for your next trip

    Once a year, on the date of the first trip Dually recorded, every trip starts with a party popper's pop and the mascot holds a party over Home. If you don't drive that day, the party now comes on the first day you do drive in the week after, with the pop on every trip that day, as it would have had on the date. After a week with no driving it waits for next year.

Version 0.3.1 14 October 2026

  • Improved
    The mascot in the new colours

    Dually's mascot is orange now, with the dark D of Dually's icon on his chest. When he waves, moonwalks and holds his party, the D turns on its side and becomes a grin. His soles are smoked black and light up amber whenever a foot is on the ground, like a car's lights, so they flash with his steps. Everything he does is as it was.

  • Improved
    A new name and a new look: Dually Trip Tracker

    The app is now called Dually Trip Tracker, Dually for short. Its icon is a dark D on orange, its screens are orange on a warm black, and its words are set in new typefaces. The report for your accountant has the same new look. Everything the app does is as it was. Dually is a new app on your phone, beside the one you had under the earlier name, and it starts empty. To bring your trips across: in the earlier app, open Settings and press Export all data; then in Dually, open Settings, press Import data and pick that file. Every trip with its route comes across, and so does every report you sent, your work hours and what the report says about you. Then pair your truck again in Setup, type the odometer's reading again, look over the rest of Settings, and remove the earlier app, so that only one app records the truck.

  • New
    Ten more sounds to choose from

    Dually has twelve built-in sounds where it had two. Beside the chirp and the chime there are a seatbelt chime, a key fob, a turn signal, a counter bell, a coin and a sonar ping, made for the moment Dually connects to the truck, and a horn tap, an engine rev, a burnout and a race start, made for the moment the trip is under way. Either sound can use any of the twelve, or a sound you add from your phone. Every one has a Play button, so you can hear it before you choose it. Nothing changes until you choose: after this update the connect sound is still the chirp and the trip-start sound the chime, and a sound of your own that is in use stays in use.

  • Improved
    Sounds take one tile in Settings

    Settings has one short tile, Sounds, where it had two long ones. It has a row for the connect sound and a row for the trip-start sound, each with the name of the sound in use and its switch. Press a row to open that sound's own screen: there you choose the sound, play any of them, add a sound from your phone, or remove one you added. The switch at the end of a row still silences that sound and keeps your choice.

  • New
    A run across the screen when a report is sent

    When you come back from your email app and answer "I sent it", Dually's mascot runs in from the left along the foot of the Report screen, runs on the spot for two seconds with his shoes smoking, then brakes with his brake lights on and slides out to the right. Wheels whirr while he runs on the spot, and tyres squeal as he slides. The two sounds play like the phone's own screen sounds: a phone on silent or on vibrate stays silent. They are also silent when both sounds are switched off in Settings. He does not run after "Mark as sent". Everything under him can be pressed while he is there, and he is gone at once if you leave the screen.

  • Improved
    Dually stays upright

    Dually no longer turns on its side when you turn the phone. Every screen stays upright. On a tablet or an unfolded phone Android still turns it.

  • New
    Google Play may ask for a review

    In a copy of Dually installed from Google Play, Google Play may show its own review card over Dually at three moments: once Dually holds 5 trips it recorded, once it holds 100, and after you answer "I sent it" for a report. Dually asks for the card once for each of the three, and leaves 30 days between two requests. It never asks while a trip is being recorded or a vehicle is connected. Google Play decides whether the card appears, and Dually is not told whether it did or what you wrote. A copy installed from the website asks for nothing.

  • New
    A party after a year of Dually

    One year after your first recorded trip, and on that date every year after, Dually's mascot holds a small party. Each trip that starts that day begins with the pop of a party popper in place of its usual first sound, wherever the phone is. If Dually is open on Home when the trip starts, the mascot fades in in a party cap, brings a popper out from behind his back and fires it, and confetti comes down round him; if not, he does it the next time you open Dually that day. Everything under him can be pressed while he is there. The pop has no switch of its own: it is silent when both sounds are switched off in Settings.

  • New
    A second greeting: the moonwalk

    Dually's mascot has a second way to say hello. In a hat, he moonwalks in from the right, skids to a stop in the middle with his brake lights on, thrusts his hips, spins once and moonwalks off to the left. He takes turns with the wave: one time you open Dually he waves, the next he moonwalks. After this update the moonwalk comes first. As before, everything under him can be pressed while he is there.

  • Improved
    The wave, from one side of the screen to the other

    The mascot's wave has a new course. He walks in from the left, stops in the middle with his brake lights on, turns to you and waves, turns away to the right and walks on off the screen; the lights go out as he takes his first step. He used to come in from the right and fade away where he stood. He still takes turns with the moonwalk, and everything under him can be pressed while he is there.

  • Fixed
    The odometer after a reading typed before driving off

    A reading typed in the truck before you drive off no longer loses that drive. Dually starts the trip when the phone connects, so the reading was typed during the trip, and the whole trip was left out of the odometer. Now what you drive after the reading is added. If your odometer is short from a reading typed before this version, type the dashboard's figure once more.

  • New
    Email support

    Settings has a new tile, Email support. It opens your email app with a message to support@dually.top, with Dually's version and your phone's model filled in at the end. Dually is one person's project: answers come when he has time.

  • Improved
    A smaller download

    Dually is less than a third of the size it was to download: the file from the website is 12 MB where it was 40 MB, and the copy from Google Play shrinks in the same way. Code that Dually never used is no longer packed in. Nothing in the app works differently.

  • Improved
    A lower tile while a trip is recorded

    On Home, the green tile of a trip being recorded is about half as high, so today's trips start higher on the screen. It shows Recording, when the trip started with its minutes under that, the distance, and a dark square that ends the trip. The square has the stop sign and no words. The tile no longer says where the trip started; the Trips screen still does.

  • New
    Today or the month, with one press

    While a trip is recorded, press the Today card on Home to see the month's business distance, its share and its dollars. Press again for today. Two small dots show which side is up, and every new trip starts with Today.

  • Improved
    The odometer turns as you drive

    Home shows the odometer like the one on a dashboard: each digit of the whole kilometres in its own cell. During a trip in a paired vehicle the last cell is green and its digit turns slowly on to the next as you drive, one digit for every kilometre. It counts from the whole number you typed in Settings, so the last digit can change at another moment than the truck's own. When no trip is running, Home shows the same number as Settings.

Version 0.3.0 9 October 2026

  • Fixed
    The phone's date set ahead

    Dually keeps its own time. Setting the phone's date a day ahead for a few seconds no longer brings "No trip recorded today" after a day of trips, and no longer cuts a trip short or dates it tomorrow. The Log gets a line when Dually sees the date change.

  • New
    Dually waves hello

    When you open Dually, its mascot walks in along the bottom of the screen, turns to you and waves. He is in nobody's way: everything under him can be pressed while he is there.

  • Improved
    Android Auto: everything on one screen

    Dually's screen in the car no longer scrolls. It shows the trip, then today's and the month's business distance and the month's dollars, with the button under them.

  • New
    Dollars on Home's month tile

    Under the month's business distance, Home shows what it comes to at your rate, such as $289. The rate is the one in Settings, which the widget uses too.

  • Improved
    A GPS point every 2 seconds

    During a trip Dually now reads the truck's position every 2 seconds. It was every 5. The recorded route follows bends more closely.

  • Improved
    The odometer counts up as you drive

    During a trip in a paired vehicle, the odometer on Home and in Settings adds the distance as it is driven. Until now the whole trip was added when it ended.

  • Improved
    A lower truck tile on Home

    The tile for the truck's connection takes less room. The vehicle's name is on its first line, its sentences are shorter, and the drawing of the phone and the truck is smaller. It moves as before.

Version 0.2.0 9 October 2026

  • New
    The odometer on Home

    Home shows each vehicle's odometer: your last reading in Settings, plus every trip since.

  • Improved
    All of today's trips on Home

    Home lists every finished trip of today, the newest at the top, in place of the last trip alone. Its top line no longer says Home.

  • Improved
    Location, explained first

    Before Android asks you to allow location all the time, Dually says what it collects, why, and that it stays on this phone.

  • Improved
    The battery setting

    Setup's battery button opens Android's list of apps. Show all apps, choose Dually, then "Don't optimise".

  • Improved
    Buttons that stay in view

    On the welcome page and on Setup during the first start, the button to go on stays at the bottom of the screen while you scroll.

Version 0.1.0 9 October 2026

  • New
    What's new

    Settings shows the version of Dually on the phone. Tap it for this list: every version and what changed in it. After an update it opens once by itself.

  • New
    Trips start by themselves

    When the phone connects to your truck's Bluetooth, a trip starts and Dually records the drive with GPS, even with the app closed. It ends when the truck disconnects, or once the truck has stood still for 10 minutes.

  • New
    Several vehicles

    Pair each work vehicle in Setup, and any of them starts a trip. Each trip records which vehicle it was in, each vehicle has its own odometer, and the report names the vehicle when the month had more than one.

  • New
    Business or Personal

    Trips in your work hours are saved as Business, the others as Personal or not at all. Any trip can be changed, edited or added by hand on the Trips screen.

  • New
    A label for each trip

    Press a trip, then Label, and pick one of the labels you used before or type a new one. The label used where the trip ended, within 200 m, is listed first and already chosen. The report prints it as the trip's purpose.

  • New
    The monthly report

    A PDF and a CSV of the month's Business trips, with the odometer at the start and the end, handed to your email app addressed to your accountant. A reminder on the 1st, until it is sent.

  • New
    Android Auto and the home-screen widget

    The trip on the car's screen and on a widget, each with a Start or End button. The widget also shows this month's and this year's Business distance in dollars, for reference.

  • New
    Two sounds of your choosing

    One when the truck connects, one when it drives off. Each can be Dually's own sound or an audio file from the phone.

  • New
    Kilometres or miles

    Every screen, the widget and the report in the unit you choose. Trips are stored the same either way.

  • New
    Your data stays yours

    No account and no server. Export all your data to one file and import it again, and Android's own backup keeps a copy in your Google account.

  • New
    Buy me a coffee

    A tile above Version in Settings opens Dually's page on Buy Me a Coffee in the browser, for anyone who would like to say thanks. Dually stays free, with no ads.
