Dually Trip Tracker compared with TripLog, MileIQ, Driversnote and Everlance

TripLog, MileIQ, Driversnote and Everlance are established mileage services: an account, your trips in their cloud, apps for iPhone and Android, and paid plans with support behind them. Dually is a one-person project with a narrower aim: a free Android app that starts a trip when your phone connects to your work vehicle, and keeps your trips on your phone, with an optional online backup you can buy once.

From each company's own website on 8 October 2026. Plans and prices change, so check their pricing pages before you choose. Something out of date? Write to support@dually.top.

Dually fits if

  • You drive one or more work vehicles that connect to your Android phone by Bluetooth.
  • You want every trip logged without paying, and without a limit on trips.
  • You would rather not need an account, and want your trips on your phone, with a copy online only if you choose to buy one.
  • You are fine with support that is one person answering email.

Another app fits better if

  • You have an iPhone, or want a web dashboard.
  • Your company reimburses a team, and wants every driver's trips in one place.
  • You also want expenses, receipts, or help with taxes.
  • You want an app with a company and support behind it.

Dually and TripLog

TripLog is the closest of the four: it can also start a trip on your car's Bluetooth (once you pass 8 km/h), and it sorts trips by work hours. Its free Basic plan tracks without limit and limits reports; Premium adds the web app, more reports and integrations. Dually keeps it simpler and free, without needing an account. triplog.net

Dually and MileIQ

MileIQ detects drives from the phone's motion and asks you to swipe each one as Business or Personal, or sorts them by work hours. Its free plan covers 40 drives a month. It uses your car's Bluetooth to tell which vehicle you are in, not to start a drive. mileiq.com

Dually and Driversnote

Driversnote tracks by the phone's motion, or by its own iBeacon placed in the car, and has a Canadian site with a CRA logbook and prices in Canadian dollars. Its free plan reports up to 15 trips a month. driversnote.com

Dually and Everlance

Everlance is mileage plus expense and income tracking, with bank sync and tax help on its top plan. It detects trips from the phone's motion and GPS; its free plan covers 30 automatic trips a month. everlance.com
