Free automatic mileage tracker · Android · iPhone

Tired of paying subscriptions?

I was. That's why I built Dually.

Dually runs free on your phone, with every feature and no ads. It starts a trip when your phone connects to your truck, and makes the monthly mileage report for your accountant. Want an online backup, for safety? Pay once, US$19.99, and never again.

On your phone

The report for your accountant

A sample page, drawn by the app's own layout code from made-up trips.

By itself

On Android. What the iPhone does differently is below.

  • Starts a trip when the phone connects to a work vehicle's Bluetooth, even with Dually closed, notes which vehicle it was, and ends it two minutes after the vehicle disconnects. Pair as many vehicles as you drive.
  • Ends a trip when the truck has stood still for ten minutes, then starts the next one when it drives off.
  • Plays a sound you can hear even on silent when it connects, and another when the truck drives off.
  • Looks up each trip's start and end address, and saves it as Business or Personal by your work hours.
  • Keeps each vehicle's odometer from one reading, plus every trip since, and asks whether it is still right when you switch to another vehicle.
  • Reminds you to send last month's report, and tells you if a work day has no trip.
  • Tells you when an oil change or other service is due, by kilometres or months, whichever comes first: a card on Home, and a notification after a trip, never while you drive.

With you

On Android, and most of it on the iPhone too.

  • Start and End by hand on the phone, on the Android Auto screen, or on the home-screen widget.
  • A home-screen widget: the trip as it runs, Start and End, and this month's and this year's Business kilometres in dollars, at a rate you set.
  • Edit, delete or add a missed trip, and mark any trip Business or Personal, even the one you are driving.
  • A graph of the year: each month's Business and Personal kilometres, side by side.
  • A service log for each vehicle: oil changes, tire rotations and anything else, with the date, odometer, cost and a note.
  • Asked when a Business trip runs past your work hours, with a one-tap Mark Personal on the notification and on Android Auto.
  • Delete a trip while it records, if it started in the wrong vehicle, or from its edit screen.
  • Label a trip with its purpose, from the labels you used before: the one used where it ended is chosen already, and the report prints it.
  • Kilometres or miles, the report included.
  • Send the report for a month, a date range or a whole year, with a year-end summary; choose whether Personal trips and each trip's odometer are listed. Dually opens your email app with the PDF attached, and you press send.
  • Export and import all your data as one file.
  • See what changed in each version, in Settings and on the What's new page.

On iPhone

Dually for iPhone needs iOS 16 or newer. An iPhone app cannot see the truck connect by itself, so Setup walks you through two Shortcuts automations, once. From then on a trip starts when the iPhone connects to the truck's Bluetooth or CarPlay, records the drive with the app closed, and ends when the truck disconnects or has stood still for a while.

A trip in progress shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with End trip, and the truck's CarPlay screen has Start trip and End trip. The work hours, the labels, the month's report, Service and the odometer, kilometres or miles, the home-screen widget, the reminder when you seem to drive with no trip recorded, and the optional online backup (Sign in with Apple or Google) work as on Android. An export from one imports on the other.

On Android only, for now: several vehicles, the sounds, the year's graph, reports for more than one month, and the reminders to send the report or about a work day without a trip.

Installing Dually

Get Dually Trip Tracker on Google Play. It needs Android 14 or newer, and Google Play keeps it up to date.

On an iPhone, download Dually Trip Tracker on the App Store. It needs iOS 16 or newer.

Open Dually once it is installed. A first page says what it does; OK leads to Setup, which asks for the permissions and pairs your vehicles (on the iPhone, walks you through the two Shortcuts automations). Never uninstall Dually to fix a problem or to update it, because that deletes every trip.

Dually for Android is built and tested on one Xiaomi phone. On other Android phones the automatic start depends on how hard the maker stops apps in the background; Setup shows what it can check.

Your trips stay on your phone

Dually is private by default: it needs no account, and with its optional online backup off it connects to nothing of its own. Your trips leave the phone only in files you send or save yourself, in your phone's own backup (Android's, or iCloud on the iPhone), as positions handed to the phone's own address lookup (Android's, or Apple's) and, only if you buy the optional online backup, as a copy of your trips, without their GPS points, in Google's Firebase, in Montréal, Canada.

Questions

Is Dually free?

Yes. Dually is free, with every feature, no subscription and no ads. The only purchase is the optional online backup: one purchase of US$19.99 that keeps a copy of your trips, without their GPS points, in Google's Firebase, at its data centre in Montréal, Canada. It is a personal project with no support desk: you can write to support@dually.top, and its maker answers when he has time. If it is useful to you, you can buy him a coffee at buymeacoffee.com/SeaWingman.

How does Dually start a trip by itself?

You pair your vehicle's Bluetooth once, in Setup. From then on Android wakes Dually when the phone connects to it, even with the app closed, and Dually records the drive with GPS. The trip ends two minutes after the vehicle disconnects, or when it has stood still for ten minutes. On the iPhone, Setup walks you through two Shortcuts automations instead, which start a trip when the iPhone connects to the truck's Bluetooth or CarPlay and end it when it disconnects.

Does Dually need an account or the internet?

No. Every feature works without an account, and your trips stay on your phone: Dually comes with its online backup off, and with it off the app connects to nothing of its own. Only the street addresses are looked up by the phone's own address service, which on most phones uses the internet. If you buy the optional online backup, you sign in with Google (on the iPhone, with Apple or Google), and a copy of your trips, without their GPS points, is kept in Google's Firebase, in Montréal, Canada, until you delete it.

Is the report enough for the CRA or the IRS?

The report lists each trip's date, start and end times, start and end addresses, distance, Business or Personal, and its purpose: the label you gave it. It also has each vehicle's odometer at the start and end of the period. Both the CRA and the IRS ask a logbook to show the purpose of each business trip, and Dually prints one only for a trip you labelled. Ask your accountant what else your records need.

Does Dually work on iPhone?

Yes. Dually Trip Tracker on the App Store needs iOS 16 or newer. Setup walks you through two Shortcuts automations that start and end a trip when the iPhone connects to the truck's Bluetooth or CarPlay. A few things are on Android only for now: see On iPhone.

Kilometres or miles?

Either. One setting switches every distance in the app, and the report, between kilometres and miles.

Is there a home-screen widget?

Yes. It shows the trip as it is recorded, with Start and End buttons, and this month's and this year's Business kilometres in dollars, at a rate per kilometre you set in Settings (70 cents to start). The dollars are for your reference only; the report for your accountant shows none. On Android, a switch in Settings turns the whole widget off; on the iPhone, the widget's Start and End need iOS 17 or newer.

Can Dually track more than one vehicle?

On Android, yes. Pair each work vehicle in Setup, and any of them starts a trip by itself. Each trip records which vehicle it was in, each vehicle has its own odometer, and the report names the vehicle when the period had more than one. A drive in a vehicle that is not paired can be started with the Start button, or added by hand afterwards, and Dually can remind you when the phone notices you driving during work hours with nothing being recorded. The iPhone app has one vehicle for now.

Can I note what each trip was for?

Yes. Press a trip on the Trips screen, then Label, and pick a label you used before or type a new one. The label last used where the trip ended, within 200 m, is listed first and already chosen, so a regular stop takes one press of Save. The report prints the label as the trip's purpose.

Is Dually on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes: Dually Trip Tracker on Google Play and on the App Store, which install it and keep it up to date.

How Dually compares

TripLog, MileIQ, Driversnote and Everlance are complete services, with an account, a cloud and a web dashboard. Dually is narrower: free, on Android and iPhone, no account needed, and your trips stay on your phone. See the comparison.
